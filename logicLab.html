🕵️ Your next mission: design your own mystery

Create a logic-grid investigation for another team to solve. You can reuse one of the encryption methods from this activity or invent your own classroom-safe code.

1. Create the mystery
Choose your people, places and objects. Decide the one correct arrangement.
2. Write fair clues
Include direct clues, negative clues and at least one clue requiring deduction.
3. Encrypt evidence
Use Caesar, binary ASCII, Atbash, Morse, Vigenère or your own clearly explained cipher.
4. Build the key
Hide numbers in the correct grid positions to reveal a final code.
5. Test it
Ask a partner to solve it. Check that there is exactly one solution.
6. Improve it
Make clues clear, challenging and impossible to brute-force.
